# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People with ADHD (TDAH), primarily adults managing daily life in Brazilian Portuguese. They use Sidekick throughout the day — morning to check tasks, afternoon to track habits and medication, evening to log mood. Their context is often fragmented attention: they open the app quickly, need immediate clarity on what to do next, and rely on external reward signals to sustain momentum.

## Product Purpose

Sidekick is a gamified daily companion for people with ADHD. It combines task management, habit tracking, medication reminders, mood logging, and goal setting under one roof, with XP / coins / levels / streaks making consistency feel rewarding rather than punitive. Success looks like a user building and maintaining routines they couldn't hold before, because the app makes the next step always obvious and the reward always visible.

## Brand Personality

Warm, grounding, motivating. Sidekick is the supportive friend who remembers everything so you don't have to — calm, never judgmental, gently celebratory. It earns trust by being organized; it earns loyalty by never making users feel bad about yesterday.

## Anti-references

- **Clinical / medical apps**: cold blue palettes, hospital-white surfaces, sterile density — the app is about wellbeing, not diagnosis
- **Corporate productivity tools**: Jira / Monday energy, dense grids, corporate blues, zero personality
- **Childish / gamey**: Duolingo-style shrieking colors, bouncy mascots, kids-app aesthetic — gamification should feel mature and earned
- **Minimalist / plain**: ultra-clean no-color checklist apps with no soul — Sidekick has warmth and identity

## Design Principles

1. **Calm over chaos** — ADHD minds are already busy; every screen should reduce ambient anxiety, not add to it. One clear action per view. Short labels, not paragraphs.
2. **Companion, not judge** — The tone is always with the user, not evaluating them. Incomplete tasks are opportunities, not failures. No red-alert shaming.
3. **Structure that motivates** — XP, streaks, and progress bars are the backbone of engagement. They must feel earned and precise, not manufactured or hollow.
4. **Progress is always visible** — Forward motion — even small — should be legible at a glance. The user should always know where they stand without having to think.
5. **Notion + Headspace balance** — Clean, considered structure (Notion) with warmth and intentional calm (Headspace). Not rigid, not loose.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- **Low cognitive load** is the primary requirement: one dominant action per screen, short copy, no visual clutter
- WCAG AA contrast for all body and UI text — muted gray on near-white is not acceptable
- Reduced motion should be respected; animations are present but must not gate content or overstimulate
- Language is Brazilian Portuguese throughout
